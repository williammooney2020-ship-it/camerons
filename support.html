Common questions

The app says "No connection". Keep working. Your changes are saved on the phone and send automatically when you are back in coverage.

I can't sign in. Check your username has no spaces and ask the office to confirm your login is switched on.

I want my account or information removed. Contact the office and we will remove your login and personal details, except records the law requires us to keep.
