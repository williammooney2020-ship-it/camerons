Camerons app — Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

The Camerons app is run by Cameron's Group (Cameron Quarry, Cameron Orchards and the family farms), 2196 Old Coach Road, Pukehina 3186, New Zealand. It is a work tool for our staff, with a public section for customers and site visitors.

What the app collects

Staff (signed in):

  • Your name, username, job title, division and mobile number, entered by the office or by you.
  • Work records you enter: timesheets, clock in and out times, leave requests, rosters, tasks, prestart checks, safety reports, forms, training records, load counts and chat messages.
  • Photos and signatures you attach to prestarts, safety reports, tasks and forms.
  • Your location at the moment you clock in or out, only if "Enable GPS location" is switched on in your profile. The app does not track your location at any other time.
  • Payslip documents, if payroll uploads them. Only you and payroll staff can open them.

Public (not signed in):

  • Quote requests: the name, phone, email, company, delivery address and job details you type in.
  • Site check-in: your name, company, vehicle registration and mobile number, so we know who is on site in an emergency.

The app has no advertising and no tracking or analytics. We do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

What we use it for

To run the business: paying staff correctly, approving leave, rostering, keeping machines and sites safe, meeting our health and safety obligations, and answering quote requests.

Who can see it

Staff see their own records. Supervisors, managers and office staff see what they need for approvals, safety and payroll, according to the access the business has given them. Pay rates and payslips are limited to payroll staff.

Where it is kept

Records are stored on a secured database hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia, and a copy is kept on your phone so the app works without signal. The weather panel loads a forecast for Otamarakau from Open-Meteo; no personal information is sent to it.

How long we keep it

For as long as you work with us and for as long afterwards as New Zealand law requires us to keep employment, wage and health and safety records.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete your login and personal details; we will do so unless the law requires us to keep the record. Contact the office using the details below.
